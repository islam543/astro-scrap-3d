CYBER ZOMBIE SIEGE - PLAYER MANUAL (README)
Prepared by: Islam Al-Absi and Ahmed Abu Arrah
Project: ASTRO SCRAP: Cyber Zombie Siege

1. UNITY VERSION USED
Unity Version: Unity 6 / Unity 6000.3.13f1
Engine: Unity 3D with C# scripts
Platform: PC build for Windows / Mac


2. GAME OVERVIEW
Cyber Zombie Siege is a 3D first-person zombie survival shooter. The player is the last survivor inside a cyber-zombie infected zone. The goal is to survive two zombie waves and defeat the final Cyber Monsters2 boss.

The game uses a round-based structure:
- Round 1: Introductory zombie wave.
- Round 2: Harder zombie wave with stronger enemies.
- Round 3: Final Cyber Monsters 2 boss encounter.

3. GAME CONTROLS
W / A / S / D        Move the player
Mouse               Look around / aim
Left Mouse Button   Shoot weapon
E                   Pick up weapon
G                   Drop weapon
R                   Restart after game over
Esc                 Pause / open pause menu, if available

4. GAME OBJECTIVES
The main objective is to survive all enemy rounds and complete the mission. The player must move around the arena, aim carefully, shoot enemies, manage health, and use weapon pickups when needed.

Objectives by round:
- Clear all zombies in Round 1.
- Clear all stronger zombies in Round 2.
- Defeat the Cyber Monsters 2 boss in Round 3.
- Stay alive until the victory screen appears.

5. HOW TO WIN
The player wins by defeating every enemy in the full game sequence:
1. Kill all Round 1 zombies.
2. Kill all Round 2 zombies.
3. Defeat the Cyber Monsters 2 boss.
4. Reach the Mission Complete / Victory screen.

6. HOW TO LOSE
The player loses if their health reaches zero. When this happens, the game shows the Game Over screen and the player can press R to restart the scene.

7. SPECIAL MECHANICS AND FEATURES
- Random Zombie Spawning: Zombies spawn randomly from multiple predefined spawn points around the arena. This makes each playthrough less predictable and forces the player to watch all directions.
- Round Progression: The GameManager starts Round 1, then Round 2, then the boss round after all enemies in the current round are defeated.
- Enemy Scaling: Round 2 zombies have higher health, speed, and attack pressure than Round 1 zombies. The boss has much higher health and acts as the final challenge.
- Weapon Pickup System: The player can pick up weapons using E and drop weapons using G.
- First-Person Combat: The player aims with the mouse and shoots using the left mouse button.
- HUD Display: The HUD shows important survival information such as current round, player health, zombies left, and score.
- Mission Board: The mission board explains the objective and controls before or during gameplay.
- Victory and Game Over Flow: The game has clear end states for winning and losing.

8. SCORING SYSTEM
- Standard zombie killed: +10 points
- Round 2 zombie killed: +20 points
- Defeating the Cyber Monsters 2 boss: +200 points
- Completing Round 1 without losing a life: +50 bonus points
- Surviving with full health at the end of a round: +25 bonus points

9. BASIC PLAYING INSTRUCTIONS
1. Start the game from the main menu.
2. Read the mission board to understand the controls and objective.
3. Use WASD to move and the mouse to aim.
4. Shoot zombies before they get close enough to attack.
5. Pick up weapons when needed using E.
6. Keep moving because zombies can spawn from different directions.
7. Clear Round 1 and Round 2.
8. Defeat the Cyber Monsters 2 boss to win.
9. If you die, press R to restart.

10. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
Minimum:
- OS: Windows 10 / Windows 11 or macOS supported by the Unity build
- CPU: Dual-core processor or better
- RAM: 4 GB
- GPU: DirectX 11 / OpenGL-compatible graphics card
- Input: Keyboard and mouse
- Storage: At least 1 GB free space

Recommended:
- OS: Windows 10 / Windows 11
- CPU: Quad-core processor
- RAM: 8 GB or more
- GPU: Dedicated graphics card for smoother 3D gameplay
- Input: Keyboard and mouse

11. NOTES FOR PLAYERS
Cyber Zombie Siege is a short single-player survival experience designed for a course project demonstration. The expected playtime for one successful run is around 5 to 10 minutes depending on player skill and enemy balancing.
